Warning signs

Something may be leaking if you notice this.

Visible water is not the only warning sign. Changes in your walls, floors, water usage or plumbing performance can also indicate a problem.

  • Water stains on walls or ceilings
  • Damp or musty smells
  • Unexplained increase in water usage
  • Dripping taps or exposed pipes
  • Water pooling near fixtures
  • Peeling paint or damaged surfaces
  • Unusual sounds behind walls
  • Low or inconsistent water pressure
Do not ignore recurring moisture.

If a leak keeps returning after a temporary fix, the actual source may still be active. Contact the FlowForge team for a proper inspection.

Leak repair FAQ

Questions customers usually ask.

How do I know if I have a hidden leak?

Damp patches, unexplained water usage, musty smells, unusual sounds and recurring moisture can all be signs. An inspection is the best way to understand the cause.

Can you repair leaks behind walls?

Our team can inspect accessible plumbing and recommend the appropriate repair approach. The required work depends on the location, pipe condition and access.

Should I turn off the water supply?

If water is actively leaking and it is safe to do so, turning off the nearest isolation valve or main water supply can help reduce further damage while you arrange assistance.

Do you handle apartment and office leaks?

Yes. FlowForge supports homes, apartments, offices, shops, restaurants and other commercial properties across Bengaluru.
