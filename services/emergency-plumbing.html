Emergency plumbing FAQ

Common questions before you call.

What should I do before the plumber arrives?

If it is safe to do so, turn off the nearest isolation valve or the main water supply. Move valuable items away from the affected area and avoid using electrical equipment near water.

Do you handle emergency plumbing at night?

Our emergency line is available 24/7. Call the emergency number so we can understand the situation and confirm availability.

Can you help with a blocked drain?

Yes. We handle slow drains, serious blockages, drain backups and related pipe problems for residential and commercial properties.

Will I receive a price before work begins?

We explain the diagnosis and recommended work before proceeding. The final estimate may depend on the cause, access and parts required.
