Drain cleaning FAQ

Questions about blocked drains.

Why does my drain keep blocking?

Recurring blockages may be caused by buildup, damaged pipes, poor drainage flow, excessive waste or an issue further along the drainage line.

Can you help with a kitchen sink blockage?

Yes. We handle kitchen sink blockages, slow drainage, unpleasant smells and related pipe or trap issues.

Is a blocked drain an emergency?

It depends on the severity. An overflowing drain, sewage backup or blockage affecting multiple fixtures should be treated as urgent and addressed quickly.

Do you provide commercial drain cleaning?

Yes. FlowForge supports restaurants, offices, apartment communities, shops and other commercial properties.
