1. Information we collect

When you contact FlowForge Plumbing or submit a booking request, we may collect information that helps us respond to your request and provide plumbing services.

Information you provide

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service address or locality
  • Preferred date and time
  • Details about your plumbing problem

Information collected automatically

Basic technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate location and website usage data may be collected through analytics or hosting tools.

2. How we use information

We use submitted information for legitimate business and service purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and booking requests
  • Confirming appointment details
  • Providing estimates or service information
  • Communicating about an active service request
  • Improving the website and customer experience
  • Maintaining basic business and service records

We do not use your contact information for unrelated marketing without an appropriate reason or permission.

3. Information sharing

We may share necessary information with service providers who help us operate the website, process enquiries, send emails or manage hosting and analytics.

We do not sell your personal information. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, legal process or to protect the safety and rights of customers, staff or the business.

4. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should avoid submitting sensitive information that is not required for your plumbing enquiry.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences, understand website usage and improve performance.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect certain website features.

6. Your choices

You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold, request correction of inaccurate information or ask us to stop using information for a particular communication purpose.

Some information may need to be retained for legal, accounting, safety or legitimate business purposes.

7. Children’s privacy

This website is intended for adults and businesses seeking plumbing services. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

8. Policy updates

We may update this policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
