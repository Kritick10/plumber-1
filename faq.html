General questions

What areas does FlowForge serve?

We serve homes and businesses across Bengaluru, including Whitefield, Electronic City, Koramangala, Indiranagar, HSR Layout, Marathahalli and nearby areas.

What type of properties do you work with?

We work with houses, apartments, offices, restaurants, retail stores, apartment communities and other commercial properties.

Do you provide emergency plumbing support?

Yes. Our emergency line is available for urgent issues such as burst pipes, serious leaks, overflowing fixtures, drain backups and water-related emergencies.

Booking and service

How do I book a plumber?

You can call us, send a WhatsApp message or complete the booking form on our website. We will collect the details and confirm the next available service time.

How quickly can a plumber arrive?

Response time depends on your location, the urgency of the issue and technician availability. For urgent problems, call our emergency line so we can assess the situation.

Can I choose a preferred time?

Yes. When submitting a booking request, you can share your preferred date and time. We will confirm availability with you.

What should I do before the plumber arrives?

If it is safe, turn off the nearest water isolation valve or the main supply. Move valuable items away from the area and avoid electrical equipment near water.

Pricing and estimates

Do you provide estimates before work begins?

We explain the problem, recommended solution and expected work before proceeding. The final estimate may depend on access, parts, labour and the condition of the plumbing.

Is emergency plumbing more expensive?

Emergency work can involve different timing, access and response requirements. We explain the applicable charges before starting the work.

Do you supply replacement parts?

We can recommend and supply suitable parts depending on the repair. The parts and installation details will be explained before the work is completed.

Specific plumbing problems

Can you repair hidden water leaks?

We can inspect visible symptoms and accessible plumbing areas to help identify the likely source. The required repair depends on the location and condition of the pipe.

Why does my drain keep blocking?

Repeated blockages can be caused by buildup, damaged pipes, poor drainage flow, excessive waste or an issue further along the drainage line.

Do you repair water heaters?

Yes. We provide water heater inspection, repair, replacement and plumbing connection support.

Do you handle complete bathroom plumbing?

Yes. Our services include bathroom fixture connections, pipe work, drainage, water supply and repair support.
